### About me
I'm an **AI Automation Engineer** with an Industrial Engineering background. I like thinking about systems, whether they are production lines or LLM pipelines, and I bring that same lens to AI: not just whether something can be automated, but whether it should be, and how to measure if it's actually better.

### What I'm working on
• Designing and experimenting with LLM-based agents that automate repetitive workflows
• Exploring self-hosted AI infrastructure, running and fine-tuning models outside the big-cloud ecosystem
• Building small game development side-projects where AI drives gameplay and decision-making
• Documenting daily progress in [daily-notes](https://github.com/onurkesmez/daily-notes)

### Featured projects

**[AI Tic Tac Toe](https://github.com/onurkesmez/ai-tic-tac-toe)** — an unbeatable AI opponent built with the minimax algorithm. [Live demo](https://onurkesmez.github.io/ai-tic-tac-toe/)

**[AI Snake Game](https://github.com/onurkesmez/ai-snake-game)** — classic Snake played by a greedy pathfinding AI. [Live demo](https://onurkesmez.github.io/ai-snake-game/)

**[AI Haiku Generator](https://github.com/onurkesmez/ai-haiku-generator)** — a Markov chain that writes original 5-7-5 poems. [Live demo](https://onurkesmez.github.io/ai-haiku-generator/)

**[daily-notes](https://github.com/onurkesmez/daily-notes)** — a running log of what I learn about AI, automation, and software, one day at a time.
